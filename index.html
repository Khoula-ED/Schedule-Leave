How changes work

  • This page is the roster. A change counts only once it appears here, and attendance follows the page.
  • Swaps are like for like and agreed by both doctors. Resus in-charge and supervisor slots go only to someone qualified for that role.
  • Ask at least 48 hours ahead, except in an emergency. Requests go to the rota maker, who approves and publishes.
  • Residents' swaps also go through the programme, as duty hours must be checked.
